CRIP Clinical Review Guidance — Chest Pain Observation

Purpose:
This fictional guidance document is created only for the CRIP proof-of-concept.
It is not an official clinical guideline and must not be used for patient care.

Clinical review should examine whether the patient record clearly documents:

1. Presenting symptoms and symptom characteristics.
2. Relevant cardiovascular comorbidities and risk factors.
3. Electrocardiogram findings.
4. Initial and repeat troponin results when documented.
5. Observation status and documented monitoring events.
6. Medications administered during the encounter.
7. The documented discharge assessment and disposition.
8. Follow-up instructions contained in the patient record.

Reviewers should compare clinical findings with the original patient record
and identify whether evidence is sufficient to support each documented finding.

The review system must not independently diagnose acute coronary syndrome,
myocardial infarction, or another condition.

The review system must not recommend treatment, testing, admission, discharge,
or other patient-care actions.

All AI-generated findings are advisory and require qualified human validation.

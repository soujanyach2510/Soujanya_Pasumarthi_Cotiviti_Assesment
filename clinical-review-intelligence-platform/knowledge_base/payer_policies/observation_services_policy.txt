HealthFirst Demo Plan
Outpatient Observation Services Policy

Policy ID: HFD-OBS-001
Version: 1.0
Status: Demonstration Policy for CRIP POC

=====================================================
1. PURPOSE
=====================================================

This policy describes the documentation elements reviewed for
outpatient observation-service claims.

It is used to help determine whether the submitted claim and
supporting encounter documentation contain enough information
for a human reviewer to evaluate the billed observation service.


=====================================================
2. SCOPE
=====================================================

This policy applies to outpatient facility claims submitted for
observation services.

For this CRIP demonstration case, the policy applies to:

Claim Type:
Outpatient Facility

Service Category:
Observation Services

Place of Service:
Hospital Outpatient

Service Code:
OBS-001


=====================================================
3. OBSERVATION SERVICE REVIEW ELEMENTS
=====================================================

When reviewing an observation-service claim, the submitted
documentation should provide enough information to identify:

1. The reason for the patient's encounter.

2. The documented condition or symptoms being evaluated.

3. The date and time the observation service began.

4. Diagnostic testing or monitoring performed during the
   encounter.

5. Relevant documented test or monitoring results.

6. Continued monitoring or reassessment during the observation
   period, when documented.

7. The patient's final disposition, such as discharge,
   admission, or transfer.

8. Documentation showing that the billed observation service
   corresponds to an event in the submitted encounter record.


=====================================================
4. CLAIM AND DOCUMENTATION ALIGNMENT
=====================================================

The reviewer should compare the billed observation service with
the supporting encounter documentation.

The documentation should allow the reviewer to identify that an
observation event occurred and should provide enough context to
understand the related encounter.

The reviewer may compare:

- the billed service
- the service date
- the place of service
- the primary diagnosis
- the observation event
- diagnostic testing
- monitoring or reassessment
- the final disposition

The information should be reviewed for consistency across the
claim and submitted encounter documentation.


=====================================================
5. DOCUMENTATION CONCERNS
=====================================================

The following situations may require additional human review:

- an observation service is billed but no observation event is
  documented

- the service date does not match the encounter documentation

- the observation start time is missing or unclear

- claim information conflicts with the encounter documentation

- the billed service cannot be clearly connected to the
  submitted encounter record

- final disposition information is missing when it is needed to
  understand the observation episode

- documentation contains conflicting dates, times, or service
  information


=====================================================
6. REVIEW CONSIDERATIONS
=====================================================

A documented observation event should be reviewed together with
the other submitted information.

The presence of an observation event by itself does not establish
that all claim-review requirements have been satisfied.

The reviewer should consider the available claim information,
encounter documentation, relevant clinical information, and any
applicable coding guidance before making a final determination.


=====================================================
7. FINAL REVIEW
=====================================================

The final claim determination is made by a qualified human
reviewer after evaluating the available claim documentation and
applicable review criteria.
